Delivery notes

EmailJS must be configured with a template accepting to_email, to_name, from_name, reply_to, subject, message, and html_message. The template recipient must be {{to_email}}. The sender's actual email address is determined by your connected EmailJS service.

History shows requests accepted by EmailJS, not guaranteed inbox delivery. Use a verified sending domain and provide unsubscribe information in marketing emails.
